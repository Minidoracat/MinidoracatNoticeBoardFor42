# Server rules

Short version: play like other people exist. The full list is five lines long,
so there is no excuse for not reading it.

1. **No griefing.** Do not destroy, loot or wall in someone else's base. Doors
   you did not build are not yours to open.
2. **No cheating.** Item spawners, mod menus and speed hacks are a permanent
   ban on the first offence. So is sharing an account with someone who uses
   them.
3. **Safe zones are PvP free.** The spawn town and the trade post are safe.
   Everywhere else you fight at your own risk and we will not restore losses.
4. **One base per group.** Mark it with a sign at the entrance. If a base has
   been abandoned for three weeks, it can be claimed by someone else.
5. **Keep chat usable.** Slurs, harassment and spam are removed on sight;
   repeat offenders are removed with them.

Breaking a rule costs you your character, your base or your account, in that
order. Admins do not negotiate in chat - if you disagree with a decision, take
it to the Discord.

> Maintenance is announced in this notice board and in Discord at least one
> hour ahead. Log out during maintenance: a character left online can lose
> gear or the whole save slot.

---

Notes for the admin editing this file, delete them before you publish:

This file is named `30_server_rules.only.txt`. The `.only` segment means it is
**never** laid out as a base copy for other languages - only players reading
this language see it. Drop the `.only` part if you would rather have every
language fall back to this text when it has no translation of its own.

Keep the file name identical in every language folder that carries a
translation, and remember that renaming it makes the notice unread again for
everyone who can see it.

Do not decorate the rules with emoji. The game draws text from pre-baked
bitmap fonts with no operating system fallback, so an emoji shows up as a
question mark or as nothing at all. Words, a PNG from `NoticeBoard/images/`,
or the shared icons of the Minidoracat UI Library are the reliable options.
